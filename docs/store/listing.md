## Full description (max 4000) – English (1424 characters)

Nutwise is two brain games in one calm, colourful app.

🔩 NUT SORT – 600 HAND-CHECKED LEVELS
Tap a bolt to lift its top nuts, tap another to drop them. Fill every bolt with one colour to win. Easy to learn, hard to master: the empty bolts are few, so plan a few moves ahead!
• 600 levels in 12 themed stages, every one checked by a solver so it can always be solved
• New ideas as you go: hidden nuts, locked and chained bolts, frozen nuts, short bolts, magnet trays, glue bolts and move-limit challenges
• Hint, Undo and Extra Bolt when you get stuck
• Earn up to 3 stars per level and open stage chests

🧠 WIT TWIST – 100 TRICK PUZZLES
The obvious answer is usually wrong! Drag words out of the question, move the hole instead of the ball, wire up logic gates and more.
• Puzzles for kids, for everyone and for engineers
• Riddles, lateral thinking, circuits, gears, code and number puzzles
• Free hints when you need a nudge

🎮 MORE WAYS TO PLAY
• Daily Challenge – one new puzzle every day
• Blueprint – copy the drawing with the nuts
• Rush Hour – solve as many as you can in 2 minutes
• Zen Garden – endless relaxing puzzles, no timer

✨ MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Smooth, sharp graphics on every phone
• Satisfying sounds and gentle vibration
• Colour-blind symbols
• English and Hindi
• Plays offline – no internet needed
• No account needed to play

Train your brain on the bus, on a break or before bed. How wise are you?
