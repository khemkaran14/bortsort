Nutwise – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Nutwise (“the app”) is a puzzle game with two games inside: a nut-sorting puzzle and Wit Twist trick puzzles. It is published by Divyrs Systems (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: you can play everything without an account, and we do not sell your data.

1. Information stored only on your phone

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, skins, puzzle progress), your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, colour-blind symbols, reminders) and your daily streak are saved on your device. They are not sent to us. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Optional account

Some versions of the app let you log in. Logging in is optional. If you choose to:

  • Phone number or email address – used to send you a one-time login code and to identify your account.
  • Sign in with Google – we receive your Google account ID, email address and name from Google, only to log you in.
  • Progress summary – your highest level, puzzles solved and coin total, so your account and any subscription work on any phone.
  • Subscription records – the plan and dates of any ad-free subscription.

Login codes are sent through an SMS or email provider acting on our behalf. We do not use this information for marketing and do not sell or rent it.

3. Ads

Some versions of the app may show our own promotional banners or full-screen ads, managed by us. We count how many times each ad is shown and tapped, without linking these counts to you. Tapping an ad opens the advertiser’s web page in your browser; that website has its own privacy policy. The app does not use third-party advertising networks and does not use your device’s advertising ID. Subscribers do not see ads.

4. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules at most one reminder a day on your phone (for the Daily Challenge or your streak). These are created on your device. You can switch them off in the app’s Settings or in your phone settings.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the app (hosting, SMS and email delivery), only as needed for that purpose, or when required by law.

6. Security and retention

Information sent between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Login codes are stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form and expire after 5 minutes. We keep account information while your account exists and delete it when you delete your account.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account) or on our website at [your server address]/delete-account. This removes your account, subscription records and saved progress summary from our server. Progress stored only on your phone stays there until you uninstall the app.

8. Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game. Playing does not require an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to withdraw consent. Contact us to use these rights.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
